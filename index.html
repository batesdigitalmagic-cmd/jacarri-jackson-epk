Biography

The long
way around

Jacarri Jackson makes R&B that keeps one foot in the church and the other on the block — soul melodies delivered with a rapper's cadence, written by someone who came to music after a full life in something else.

Born in Shreveport and raised in northwest Louisiana, Jackson spent his first act on a football field. He was a three-year letterman and all-state receiver at Logansport High School before signing to play Division I ball at Louisiana Tech. When the game ended, the writing started.

His debut single "In the Rain" landed in 2018, followed by the full-length The Perfect 7 in 2019 — eight tracks that set the template: warm, unhurried production, honest writing about wanting and being wanted, and a voice that slides between singing and speaking without ever announcing the switch. "Miss Cold-Hearted" and "In the Rain" remain his most-played songs.

Contagious Vibes followed in 2020, a longer and looser record that widened the range — "Hate My Guts," "Would That Be Enough" and "Man Enough to Say It" all pull from the same well of plainspoken grown-folks R&B. His new EP, Lothario, opens the latest chapter in that story. He has also stayed in demand as a featured voice, turning up on records by J.Hall, David Jermaine and Goody G.

He performs regularly across Louisiana and the Gulf South, backed by a live band as often as by tracks, and has built an audience of more than fifteen thousand who follow the music and the man behind it in roughly equal measure.

Short bio — for listings and programs

Jacarri Jackson is an R&B and soul artist from Shreveport, Louisiana. A former Division I wide receiver turned songwriter, his catalogue includes The Perfect 7 (2019), Contagious Vibes (2020), and the new EP Lothario. He also appears as a featured vocalist across the Gulf South scene.
